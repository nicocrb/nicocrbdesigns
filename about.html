I’m Nicolle Carbonell — call me Nicolle!

I’m a passionate and detail-oriented graphic and web designer based in Oklahoma, with a love for turning ideas into clean, thoughtful designs. I’m currently working toward my Bachelor of Arts in Graphic Design at Southern New Hampshire University, where I’m deepening my understanding of visual storytelling, design systems, and creative strategy. Before that, I earned an Associate of Science in Web Design & Multimedia from The Art Institute of Pittsburgh, which gave me a strong foundation in both technical skills and creative problem-solving.

Outside of school and work, you’ll usually find me sketching, illustrating, or brainstorming visual design concepts just for fun. Whether it’s doodling character ideas, experimenting with color palettes, or creating digital art pieces, I genuinely enjoy the creative process — it's not just what I do, it's who I am. My journey has been a mix of formal education and real-world experience — designing flyers for community events, volunteering with AV tech teams at churches, and helping clients one-on-one in roles that taught me patience, communication, and versatility.

I’m fluent in Spanish, skilled in Adobe Creative Suite, and always eager to grow. I’m actively looking for opportunities where I can contribute creatively, learn from others, and be part of a mission-driven team — whether remote or hybrid. Let’s connect — I’d love to help bring your ideas to life!
